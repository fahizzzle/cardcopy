CardCopy
========
Copies the photos and videos off camera SD cards into project folders
(Raw Photo\<Project>\Cam 1 ..., Raw Video\<Project>\Cam 1 ...), for example on Google Drive.

INSTALL
  1. Unzip the whole download (right-click the zip > Extract All). Do not run it from inside the zip.
  2. Double-click  Install.cmd  and press Enter.
     If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose More info > Run anyway:
     it is a plain script you can open in Notepad, not a program.
  3. Open CardCopy from the Start Menu or the Desktop.
  Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). No admin rights, nothing else to install.
  Installing a newer version over an old one keeps your settings.

FIRST RUN
  - "Raw folder": press Browse and pick the folder that holds (or should hold) Raw Photo and Raw Video.
  - Cameras: set USB Connection Mode to Mass Storage, then plug them in. Or put the cards in readers
    and switch to "SD cards" in the sidebar.
  - Tick the cards, check "What will be created", press "Create folders + copy cards".

SAFETY
  CardCopy never deletes and never overwrites anything, on the cards or at the destination.
  Cards are only read. A cancelled copy continues where it stopped the next time.

UNINSTALL
  Settings > Apps > CardCopy > Uninstall. Only the program is removed; photos, videos,
  project folders and your settings are left alone.

WHAT IS IN THE FOLDER
  python\    the official Python 3.11 runtime from python.org, with Qt (PySide6) for the window
  cardcopy\  the program itself, as readable .py files
  Cameras are shown with drawn icons (photo or video camera); "Picture" on a camera lets you use your own photo of it.
